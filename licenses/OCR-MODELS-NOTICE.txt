This application includes PP-OCRv6 small detection and recognition and
korean_PP-OCRv5 mobile recognition from RapidOCR's v3.9.2 model registry.
The character dictionaries were extracted from the corresponding ONNX
character metadata; newline encoding is normalized to UTF-8 LF.
Models are unmodified. Exact source URLs and SHA-256 hashes are recorded
in models/ocr/models.json alongside the redistributed models.
